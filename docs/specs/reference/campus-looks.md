# What PUP's campuses look like (for the In session art)

Checked 2026-11-15 for the campus scenes in `src/lib/campus/scenes/`. A first pass from a fast model listed 25 campuses with landmarks, palettes and sources; most of its "official profile" links were generic and several details were wrong (San Juan is not a three-storey converted school, Parañaque's trim is green, not maroon). Only what is listed here was confirmed, and only that is drawn. Each campus has a confidence line: **high** means a photo or an official page shows it, **medium** means the setting is confirmed by geography but no photo of the campus was found, **low** means only the campus's existence and town are confirmed.

## The shared kit

Confirmed from the Sto. Tomas gate photo: two white pillars under a maroon beam lettered in white. The branch scene uses that gate with "PUP" and the campus name (the real beam says "Polytechnic University of the Philippines", too long for 192 pixels), a two or three storey building with maroon corridor bands, and the campus's backdrop by geography. The building is generic on purpose: no branch building's face is claimed.

## Campuses

**Sta. Mesa (A. Mabini Campus).** High. The Main Building is cream with a maroon band at every floor (its cantilevered corridors), six floors, its plan "a 3-winged airplane propeller", the flag in front of it. The Mabini Obelisk is a tall white square shaft with the gold PUP star at its head, on a round planted island with maroon steps, a bust of Mabini at its front. The Pylon is three grey-white concrete slabs lettered P, U, P near their tops, on a brick-red stepped plinth, at the gate on Anonas/Teresa. The lagoon sits behind a grey stone wall with an arched, stepped gable in the style of Intramuros; its water is algae green. The Makati skyline shows on the horizon; the PNR line and its Santa Mesa station run by the campus, and the NLEX Connector viaduct now runs over the PNR line (left out of the strip to keep it calm). Not confirmed: the "dome" the first pass described (a "main-building-dome" label exists on a student map, its shape is unknown), so the scene draws a plain core on the roof.

**San Juan (Addition Hills).** High. A tall building whose two wings of window grids and brown piers meet at a brick-red centre panel with the gold PUP star over the university's name, a glass lobby at its foot. Residential street with wires (setting: Addition Hills, from pup.edu.ph).

**Parañaque (Sto. Niño).** High. Four floors, cream, stacked dark green panels up the middle, a pediment over the entrance, an open steel frame on the roof; a cream front wall with green posts and "PUP Parañaque Campus" in steel letters. The NAIA flight path the first pass mentioned is not confirmed and not drawn.

**Taguig (Lower Bicutan).** Medium. Ten hectares on Gen. Santos Avenue; Building A and the Cayetano Building; Zonta Park. No photo found, so the buildings are the kit's and the scene shows two buildings and a park.

**Quezon City (Commonwealth).** High for the pylon and trees. A small three-slab PUP pylon under a heavy tree canopy (pup.edu.ph photo). Building names (Rothlehner, Misereor) from the first pass are not confirmed; the buildings are drawn as a plain banded block behind the trees.

**Caloocan (Deparo, North Caloocan).** Low for the look, high for the facts: a six-storey building, 1,500 sq m a floor, planned for 2025. The finished facade is not confirmed, so it is plain white with grey fins.

**Mariveles (Bataan).** Medium. The campus is in the Freeport Area of Bataan in Mariveles (pup.edu.ph); Mt Mariveles, Bataan's highest point, stands over the town and the bay. The gate says "PUP Bataan", the campus's own name.

**Sto. Tomas (Batangas).** High. The gate photo above, a white multi-storey building and palms inside, Mt Makiling's ridge behind ("at the foot of Mount Makiling").

**Ragay (Camarines Sur).** High for the hill and the building, medium for the gulf. On a hill over the town (pup.edu.ph); the building is the white four-storey block on its campus page; Ragay lies on Ragay Gulf.

**Talisay (Batangas).** Medium. The town is on Taal Lake's north shore under the Tagaytay ridge; the scene puts the lake, a small volcano island and the ridge behind the kit. No campus photo.

**Cabiao (Nueva Ecija).** Medium. San Roque, Cabiao (pup.edu.ph); rice plain with a lone peak for Mt Arayat across the border in Pampanga.

**Calauan, Bansud.** Medium. Fields or hills with a mountain behind (Laguna's volcanoes; Mindoro's interior range). Not tied to a named peak.

**Mulanay, Unisan, Sablayan.** Medium. Coastal towns; the scene uses the sea backdrop.

**Pulilan, Sta. Maria, Maragondon, Alfonso, Biñan, San Pedro, Sta. Rosa, Lopez, Leyte.** Low. Existence and town confirmed (pup.edu.ph); backdrop by region (Bulacan plain, eastern Bulacan hills, Cavite uplands, Laguna cities, a Quezon town). Leyte was not in the first pass at all.

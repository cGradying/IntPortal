Deck: a pile on a turning table

The closest to a card game's feel. The whole session sits on a slowly turning dithered swirl; cards are dealt up from a face-down pile, sway, lean toward your finger and flip. Right answers burst and fly to the kept pile, misses shake and tuck back under the deck. The score panel reads RIGHT × COMBO into SCORE, and the swirl speeds up and brightens at each combo tier (3, 6, 10 in a row).

Play both. A keeps the swirl on the whole table and is louder; B keeps the room calm, with the swirl only in the score panel, the card backs and the pile.

A: Full tableThe swirl is the table, always on, bolder at every combo tier.

Try it

Flash: tap the card, then rate it (Good and Easy count as right, Again as a miss, Hard keeps your combo). Type: see the meaning, type the term. Choice: see the term, pick the meaning. Three in a row is x2, six is x3, ten is x4, so x4 only shows on the 12 card deck with no misses (Cards: 12 is for that).

Reduce Motion: no travel, tilt, sway, shake or particles, one still swirl frame, and a short fade for each card. Sound: one semitone up per answer in a row.

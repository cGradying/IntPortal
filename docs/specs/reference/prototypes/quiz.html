Deck: a pile on a turning table

The closest to a card game's feel. The whole session sits on a slowly turning dithered swirl; cards are dealt up from a face-down pile, sway, lean toward your finger and flip. Right answers burst and fly to the kept pile, misses shake and tuck back under the deck. The score panel reads RIGHT × COMBO into SCORE, and the swirl speeds up and brightens at each combo tier (3, 6, 10 in a row).

Play both. A keeps the swirl on the whole table and is louder; B keeps the room calm, with the swirl only in the score panel, the card backs and the pile.

A: Full tableThe swirl is the table, always on, bolder at every combo tier.
B: Card on calmDot-grid room. The swirl lives only on the card backs, the pile and the score panel.

Flash: tap the card, then rate it (Good and Easy count as right, Again as a miss, Hard keeps your combo). Type: see the meaning, type the term. Choice: see the term, pick the meaning. Three in a row is x2, six is x3, ten is x4, so x4 only shows on the 12 card deck with no misses (Cards: 12 is for that).

Reduce Motion: no travel, tilt, sway, shake or particles, one still swirl frame, and a short fade for each card. Sound: one semitone up per answer in a row.

Run: clarity and momentum

The closest to Gizmo and Duolingo. A chunky progress bar steps once per card, a combo meter fills toward the next tier and stamps x2, x3, x4, and LAR answers every tap. Answers are big tiles that press in. Play a full run in each mode, miss a card to see it come back, then read the results.

Two ways to hold LAR: big at the top and talking, or tiny in the HUD so the card gets the room. Pick one; the controls on the right change both phones.

A: CompanionLAR sits big at the top and says a short line after every answer.
B: FocusLAR is small in the HUD and the card and tiles get the space.

Try it

Score is right answers times the combo: x1, x2 at three in a row, x3 at six, x4 at ten. A miss drops the combo to x1, marks the bar and sends the card to the back of the pile. No lives. In Flash, Hard keeps the combo but does not add a step.

Reduce Motion drops slides, flips, shakes, particles and the LAR hop; a short fade stays. Sound is off by default, climbs a semitone per combo step, and (as in the app) stays quiet under Reduce Motion.

Heat: it starts calm and earns its spectacle

The table is the app's quiet room at x1. Every combo tier heats it: dots drift, a dithered swirl bleeds in from the edges, the card sways, the score gets louder, and x4 shakes and bursts. A miss cools it at once. Two ways to earn the heat. Play both, in any mode.

A: Heat by comboThe table heats with your run of right answers: x1, x2, x3, x4.
B: Heat by the clockThe table heats as you answer quickly. A soft ring counts down each card; running out is never a miss, a quick answer is just +1.

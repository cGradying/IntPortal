## 1. What this is

IntPortal Web is an **unofficial, independently-built student tool**. It is **not
affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by the Polytechnic University
of the Philippines (PUP)** or its Student Information System (SIS8). "PUP" and any PUP
marks belong to their respective owners; this service uses them only to describe what
it interoperates with.

The service signs into SIS8 **as you**, using SIS credentials you supply, to read data
you already have a right to see under your own account: your schedule, your grades,
and material derived from them. It does not access, request, or store any other
student's data.

## 2. Your account and responsibility

- You must be the legitimate holder of the PUP student account you use.
- You are responsible for your own conduct under **PUP's own Terms of Use** for SIS8;
  this service does not change or override those terms, and using this service does not
  excuse a violation of them.
- Do not share your account with anyone else, use this service to automate access to
  another student's account, or use it to bulk-collect or redistribute SIS content.

## 3. Storing your credentials

- Providing your SIS credentials to sign in is required to use the service at all.
- **Storing** them for later (so you don't need to sign in every visit — "remember me")
  is optional and off unless you turn it on.
- If you do turn it on, they are encrypted using a key derived from your own password
  (see `PRIVACY.md` and `THREAT-MODEL.md` for exactly how, and what that does and does
  not protect against). If you're unsure, leave it off — you'll just sign in more often.

## 4. No warranty on the data shown

Schedule, grades, and any AI-generated summary or study material shown here are for
your convenience only. **SIS8 is always the source of truth.** This service may show
stale, incomplete, or incorrectly parsed data if SIS8 changes its page structure or if
a scrape fails partway. Do not rely on this service for enrollment decisions, deadline
tracking with real consequences, or anything where being wrong matters — check SIS8
directly for anything that matters.

## 5. AI features and your own API key

AI features (the assistant, note summarization, quiz generation, syllabus extraction)
require you to supply your own Anthropic API key. You are billed directly by Anthropic
for that usage under Anthropic's own terms; this service does not mark up, resell, or
subsidize that cost, and does not see your billing information. Content you send
through AI features (your notes, schedule context, prompts) is transmitted to
Anthropic to generate a response — see `PRIVACY.md` for exactly what that includes.

## 6. Acceptable use

You may use this service only for your own account and your own study purposes. You
may not: share or sell access to your account; attempt to access another student's
data through this service or otherwise; attempt to circumvent SIS8's own
authentication; scrape or automate this service beyond normal interactive use;
redistribute SIS content obtained through this service.

## 7. Termination and deletion

You may stop using the service and delete your account at any time; deletion removes
every stored row and file associated with your account (see `PRIVACY.md`, Deletion).
This service may suspend or terminate access for a violation of these terms, or if
continuing to operate the service becomes impractical (e.g., a change on SIS8's side
that breaks the ability to sign in on your behalf).

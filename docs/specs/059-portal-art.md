# 059 The portal, alive: a new art style for the website's hero

**Status:** draft (the owner's ask, 2026-11-20, during 058: "for the portal make it dynamic too: instead of a normal 3D, add an effect for it, like the same concept of the chatbox where it becomes a sculpture, or try a different art style that really fits the system. You can do this later on other specs")

## What changes for the student

The portal on the website's first screen stops being a plain 3D frame of tiles. It moves and changes like something alive, in a look that belongs to IntPortal's own world.

## Direction and references

- **The chat's crystal (spec 046 C1d, `CRYSTAL_FS`, `neuronForm`, `rootPull`).** In LAR's chat the crystal shifts between forms while he thinks and grows roots when he answers. The portal could do the same: its tiles gather into forms, such as a sculpture, the campus or LAR's crystal, then fall back into the frame.
- **Other styles to try**, each as a mockup before any build:
  - dithered voxels that assemble;
  - an ASCII or Pixelify glyph field;
  - a stained-glass maroon and gold window.
- The dive and the dot ground (057 C6) stay. Whatever the portal becomes, it still dives into the page.

## Open questions

- Should it react to the student, through the pointer, tilt, scroll or LAR's taps (058 C12)?
- Should the forms tell the six stories of 058 C10, or stay abstract?
- How much does it cost on a low-end phone? 057 C2's gate and the still fallback hold.

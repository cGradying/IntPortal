# The reel: review

Spec 054 C6. Every shot is scored on a contact sheet, out of 10. The worst shots are fixed, and the sheet is scored again until every shot is 8 or more. Then comes the full render.

The rubric comes from the October film:
- Is it clear in one look?
- Does something new happen every 2 to 4 seconds?
- Is it cut to the beat?
- Is it the real app, with nothing faked?
- Is the craft good: type, spacing, no clipping?

## Sound

The score is made in code (`score.mjs`): 112 BPM, A minor. The dive drops on a downbeat at 5 s, the arp comes in with the app tour, the promise is half time, and the end card rings the home chord with two bells. Mixed to −14.1 LUFS with peaks at −1.5 dBFS. There is no voice-over, so the reel works muted, and the captions are burned in.
